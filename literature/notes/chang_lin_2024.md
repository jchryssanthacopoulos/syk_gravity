# Chang & Lin (2024) — Holographic covering and the fortuity of black holes

**Citation.** C.-M. Chang, Y.-H. Lin, *Holographic covering and the fortuity of black holes*, arXiv:2402.10129.
File: `papers/chang_lin_2024.pdf`. (Read: Secs. 1–3.1.)

## Main content
- Holographic covering: a large-N "covering" Hilbert space H̃ with the finite-N theory as a quotient;
  long exact sequence (eq. 2.5).
- **Definitions** (Sec. 2.3): a BPS operator is *monotone* if its lift gives nontrivial cohomology classes at all
  larger N (infinite sequence, eq. 2.10); *fortuitous* if Q-closedness relies on finite-N relations, so the
  sequence terminates at some N_max (eq. 2.11). Fig. 1: fortuitous states acquire anomalous dimension when N is
  continued.
- Conjecture 1: H*(H̃) ≅ perturbative BPS Fock space. Conjecture 2: monotone ↔ smooth horizonless geometries;
  fortuitous ↔ typical black-hole microstates.
- Case studies: N=4 SYM (LLM quantization) and symmetric orbifolds (LM geometries).

## Relationship to our project
Conceptual origin of "fortuity". CV adapt it to SYK (no infinite-N Hilbert space). There, fortuity is
automatic from R-charge concentration, and CV instead ask about uplift along *walks* with increasing charge.
