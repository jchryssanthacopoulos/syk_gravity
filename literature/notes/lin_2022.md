# Lin (2022) — The bulk Hilbert space of double scaled SYK

**Citation.** H. W. Lin, *The bulk Hilbert space of double scaled SYK*, arXiv:2208.07032v4 [hep-th], 21 Aug 2023
(the journal reference is not given in the PDF and has not been checked). File: `papers/lin_2022.pdf`
(31 pp). Read in full on 2026-09-30; App. A–B were skimmed.

## Main question
Can the bulk Hilbert space of holography be constructed explicitly in double-scaled SYK (DSSYK), by slicing chord
diagrams? And what is the bulk-to-boundary map?

## Physical system
Majorana DSSYK: {ψ_i, ψ_j} = 2δ_ij, H = i^{q/2} Σ_I J_I Ψ^q_I, λ = 2q²/N, q_Lin = e^{−λ} (eq. 4). Matter operators
M_s are random s-fermion monomials with Δ = s/q (eq. 6). **N.B.** Lin's q is the fermion number of H; our p plays
that role.

## Important definitions
- **Chord rules (Fig. 1):**
  - H–H crossing: e^{−λ};
  - H–M_s crossing: e^{−2qs/N} = e^{−λΔ} (Lin writes r);
  - M_s–M_s′ crossing: e^{−2ss′/N}.
  - Footnote 1: the overlap f = |I₁ ∩ I₂| is Poisson(λ/2), and E(−1)^f = e^{−λ}.
- **Two-sided chord Hilbert space.** States |n⟩ with n the number of open chords on a slice. Transfer matrix
  T = (α† + αW)/√λ with W_n = (1 − qⁿ)/(1 − q) (eq. 11). Inner product ⟨n|m⟩ = δ_nm (q;q)_n/(1−q)ⁿ (eq. 15).
- **Length and Liouville limit.** ℓ = λn (eq. 18). The Hamiltonian is eq. 19; the triple-scaling limit gives
  Liouville with 2C = 1/λ (eqs. 21–22).
- **One particle:** states |n_L, n_R⟩, transfer matrices T_L, T_R (eq. 25), recursion for the non-orthogonal inner
  product (eqs. 26–27). **m particles:** eqs. 37–41. Bulk Hilbert space = "lattice field theory with dynamical
  length" (eq. 38).

## Main results
1. tr e^{−βH} = ⟨0|e^{−βT}|0⟩ (eq. 10). The chord Hilbert space is the analogue of the JT wormhole Hilbert space.
2. **Bulk-to-boundary map.** Gram–Schmidt of H^n|Ω⟩ gives the chord-number states (eqs. 46, 48): chord number =
   Krylov complexity for the empty wormhole. The one- and multi-particle versions are eqs. 49–52.
3. **Measuring the chord number with light probes (§4.2).**
   - Averaging a two-sided s-fermion correlator over index sets gives
     C(N,s)^{−1} Σ_I i^s Ψ^s_{I,L} Ψ^s_{I,R} = e^{−2sq n̄/N} = e^{−Δℓ} (eq. 53).
   - On multi-particle states, n̄ = (q n₀ + s₁ + q n₁ + …)/q (eq. 54).
4. **Chord number = operator size.**
   - Size operator (eq. 55): size = ½ Σ_α (1 + iψ_α^L ψ_α^R), with (size)Ψ^s_I|Ω⟩ = s Ψ^s_I|Ω⟩ (eq. 56).
   - **n̄ = size/q** (eq. 57). Derivation (eq. 58): inserting the size operator is a "red chord", and each H-chord
     crossing it contributes size q.
   - Correction (eq. 59): chords share Poisson(λ/2) fermions, so each chord's size is smeared by O(λ). The size
     distribution of Hⁿ|Ω⟩ peaks near 0, q, 2q, … with widths O(λ). Since n̄ uses size/q, these are O(1/q) effects
     in strict double scaling.
5. **Algebras.** The double-scaled algebra is Type II₁ (tr a = ⟨Ω|a|Ω⟩, eq. 62). In the triple-scaling limit it
   contains the JT gravitational algebra and SL(2,R) (eqs. 32–36, 42–43).
6. **Suggestions (fn. 15; §5 point 5).** An N=2 generalization: for m = 0 one gets N=4 Liouville QM with a BPS bound
   state. The ground-state sector of N=2 DSSYK should give a Type II₁ subalgebra.

## Limitations
- Strict double scaling (q → ∞). Size-per-chord smearing is O(λ) and is neglected; that is not negligible at our
  λ ≈ 1.3–2.3.
- Majorana, non-supersymmetric. The N=2 version is only suggested.
- The one-particle inner product is not solved in closed form.

## Relationship to our project
1. **The conceptual origin of our "size ↔ wormhole length" identification.**
   - Lin's n̄ = size/q (eq. 57) is the DSSYK statement that the operator-size distribution of the two-sided state
     equals its chord-number distribution.
   - Our BPS projector P = lim e^{−βH} is the N=2, β = ∞, fixed-charge analogue: |P⟩⟩/√d is a "BPS TFD", and w_k is
     its size spectrum.
   - The progress report's empirical match w_{2n} ≈ P_n (BLY chord-number distribution) is therefore a finite-N,
     U(N)-covariant instance of Lin's eq. 57, not a new idea in itself.
2. **Our variance formula is the exact finite-N analogue of eq. 53 with s = 1.**
   - T₂ = τ(PUPU) = ⟨⟨P|U ⊗ U^*|P⟩⟩/d is a two-sided correlator of the light probe U in the BPS TFD.
   - Averaging U over its U(N) orbit gives E[T₂] = 1 − 4⟨k(N+1−k)⟩/(N(N+1)): a two-sided light-operator correlator
     measuring size.
   - Lin notes that the s = 1 case of eq. 53 is the size operator (eq. 55); ours is its U(N)-Casimir, fixed-charge,
     exact version.
3. **Why size 2 per chord pair, and not p?** [derived sketch + numerical, 2026-09-30; see `docs/derivations.md` D3]
   - Lin's rule assigns each chord the size of one Hamiltonian term. In one-flavor N=2 SYK with odd p,
     {ψ_I, ψ_J†} = 0 unless I ∩ J ≠ ∅. So H = {Q, Q†} has at most 2p − 2 fermions (FGMS §2) and is at most
     (p−1)-body in the U(N) sense. One unit of BLY chord number (one Q–Q† pair across the cut) therefore carries
     U(N) size ≈ p − 1: **k ≈ (p−1)n**.
   - For p = 3 this is k = 2n, the empirical w_{2n} ≈ P_n. The naive "k = p·n" would put the n = 1 weight at odd
     k = 3, which vanishes exactly at half filling.
   - Checked numerically (`scripts/check_size_per_chord.py`):
     - H at p = 3 is exactly supported on k ∈ {0, 2};
     - at p = 5 the BPS projector's non-identity weight sits at k = 4 (≈ 93–95 %), with a k = 2 fraction equal to
       H's own 2-body fraction (6.0 % vs 6.2 % at N′ = 10; 6.9 % vs 6.9 % at N′ = 12).
4. **Finite-λ caveat.** Lin's eq. 59 says sizes per chord are smeared by O(λ) overlaps. At our λ = 18/N′ ≈ 1.3–2.3
   this is O(1), which is consistent with w_{2n} matching P_n only to 10–20 %.
5. **Program for the gravity dual.** Lin's construction (the chord Hilbert space as the bulk Hilbert space) plus
   BLY's N=2 version is the natural home for the decoder:
   - the decoder variance is a two-sided light-probe correlator in the BPS wormhole state;
   - the size spectrum w_k is (approximately) the wormhole-length wavefunction squared.
