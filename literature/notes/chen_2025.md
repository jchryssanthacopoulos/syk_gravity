# Y. Chen (2025) — Fortuity with a single matrix

**Citation.** Y. Chen, *Fortuity with a Single Matrix*, arXiv:2511.00790 (v2, Nov 2025).
File: `papers/chen_2025.pdf`. (Read: Secs. 1–2; Sec. 3 skimmed.)

## Main content
- Model: N×N complex fermionic matrix Ψ, Q = Tr Ψ³, H = {Q, Q̄} (eq. 1.2). Equivalently Q = (i/√2) f^{abc}ψ^aψ^bψ^c
  in an su(N) basis (eq. 2.3): an N=2 SYK model with **non-random, sparse** couplings (structure constants).
  The trace mode decouples.
- **Exactly solvable**: H = 3N(N²−1) − 9Ĉ₂ (eq. 2.8), so energies depend only on the SU(N) Casimir. BPS states are
  maximal-Casimir representations r*. Non-chaotic, non-holographic, and no R-charge concentration.
- Contains fortuitous states (not in the singlet sector; a two-matrix variant puts them in the singlet sector,
  Sec. 4). An exponential number of states become BPS at N = N* and turn null at N* − 1 (Fig. 1).
- Large-N unitary matrix integral captures the fortuitous states as eigenvalue saddles. "Following N" shows the
  saddles persist but their contributions cancel as N → N−1 (Sec. 3).

## Relationship to our project
- The "single-matrix model" of CV §6.1: bare staircase uplift with F=1, κ=0. It is the rigid, non-chaotic control
  for the conjecture "metric fragility ⇔ BPS chaos".
- Together with Miyahara–Shibuya, it shows fortuity without chaos. The decoder spectrum of this model (expected:
  degenerate, non-GUE) would be a clean negative control for research Q6.
