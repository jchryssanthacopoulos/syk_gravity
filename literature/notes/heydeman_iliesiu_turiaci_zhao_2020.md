# Heydeman, Iliesiu, Turiaci & Zhao (2020) — The statistical mechanics of near-BPS black holes

**Citation.** M. Heydeman, L. V. Iliesiu, G. J. Turiaci, W. Zhao, *The statistical mechanics of near-BPS black
holes*, arXiv:2011.01953 (v2, Jun 2021), PUPT-2621. File: `papers/heydeman_iliesiu_turiaci_zhao_2020.pdf`.
(Read: Sec. 1 and App. A; Secs. 2–5 skimmed.)

## Main question
Is there a mass gap above extremality for near-BPS black holes, and does the gravitational path integral
reproduce the extremal degeneracy?

## Main results
1. The N=4 super-Schwarzian (PSU(1,1|2)) is defined and quantized exactly (localization and canonical, Sec. 3).
   Density of states (eq. 1.3): ρ(J,E) = e^{S0}δ_{J,0}δ(E) + (continuum in sinh(2π√(2Φ_r(E−E₀(J)))) above
   E₀(J)). **Mass gap** E_gap = 1/(8Φ_r) and e^{S0} extremal states, consistent with string theory.
2. Applications to 4d N=2 supergravity in flat space and to (4,4) supergravity in AdS₃ (Secs. 4–5).
3. **Appendix A — N=2 super-Schwarzian**: extremal density per R-charge
   **ρ_ext(Q,E) = e^{S0}cos(πQ) for |Q| < 1/2** (eq. A.18; via A.16–A.17). For odd q̂ without anomaly, the gap is
   E_gap = 1/(32Φ_r q̂²), set by the multiplet Q₀ = ½ ± 1/(2q̂) (Fig. 5). Also recovered from double-scaled N=2 SYK.

  App. A for N=2).

  eq. 82) and BIY's e^{S_j} use. Any gravitational prediction for the decoder in a fixed fermion-number sector must
  use this, with e^{S0}cos(πQ) matched to our d.

  gives the count, but correlators of matter at E=0 are still dynamical (LMRS).
