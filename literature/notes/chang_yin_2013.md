# Chang & Yin (2013) — 1/16 BPS states in N=4 SYM

**Citation.** C.-M. Chang, X. Yin, *1/16 BPS States in N=4 Super-Yang-Mills Theory*, Phys. Rev. D 88, 106005
(2013), arXiv:1305.6314 (journal ref as given in CV's bibliography). File: `papers/chang_yin_2013.pdf`.
(Read: abstract, Secs. 1–2.)

## Main content
Q-cohomology formulation of 1/16-BPS operators as a relative Lie superalgebra cohomology. Complete set of
cohomology classes at infinite N, matching BPS multi-gravitons. **Conjecture** (later shown false by Chang–Lin
2022) that all 1/16-BPS operators are of multi-graviton form, and discussion of the resulting black-hole entropy
puzzle.

## Relationship to our project
Historical background for the supercharge-cohomology approach to BPS states that underlies CV's long exact
sequence and the decoder. No direct technical input.
