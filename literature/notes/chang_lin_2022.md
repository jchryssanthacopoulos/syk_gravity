# Chang & Lin (2022) — Words to describe a black hole

**Citation.** C.-M. Chang, Y.-H. Lin, *Words to describe a black hole*, arXiv:2209.06728 (v4, Feb 2023).
File: `papers/chang_lin_2022.pdf`. (Read: abstract, introduction.)

## Main content
Revamped constructive enumeration of 1/16-BPS Q-cohomology in N=4 SYM. Finds the first states **not** of
multi-graviton form, for SU(2) at relatively high energies, resolving the question raised by Chang–Yin (2013).
Proves a non-renormalization theorem for the cohomological enumeration in perturbation theory, and notes a
giant-graviton-like feature.

## Relationship to our project
Background only: the first explicit non-graviton ("black-hole") cohomology classes, which later became the
prototypical *fortuitous* states. No direct technical input to the decoder analysis.
